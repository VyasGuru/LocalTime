# Privacy Policy – LocalTime

Last updated: October 1, 2026

LocalTime shows the other person's local time next to their name in LinkedIn conversations.

## What the extension accesses
- The location shown on a LinkedIn profile (for example "Austin, Texas, United States"), read only to work out that person's time zone.
- Cities you type in yourself to set or correct someone's location.

## How it is stored
- This information is saved only in your own browser, using Chrome's local extension storage.
- Saved locations expire after 30 days. You can clear a person's saved location from the pill, or remove all data by uninstalling the extension.

## What the extension does not do
- It does not read your messages.
- It does not send any data to the developer or any third party. There are no servers, analytics, ads or tracking.
- It does not sell, share or transfer any data.

## Network requests
The only network requests the extension makes are to www.linkedin.com, using your existing logged-in session, to read the location on a profile.
